# WPEG Salary Calculator

A free, private, browser-based net salary calculator for Winnipeg, Manitoba. Enter your gross
salary and pay frequency, and it computes federal tax, Manitoba provincial tax, CPP, CPP2, and
EI using official 2026 CRA and Manitoba Finance parameters — then shows your take-home pay per
cheque with plain-English perspective on what those deductions would buy.

**Live at:** https://salary.wpeg.app

## Highlights

- **Official 2026 rates** — every bracket, credit, and ceiling verified against CRA and Manitoba
  Finance publications, with citations linked in-app
- **22 colour themes** — from Catppuccin to Vercel, each with full light and dark modes
- **"On this page" rail** — jump-menu navigation with scroll-spy on desktop
- **Private by design** — all math runs locally in your browser; nothing is stored or transmitted
- **Share & export** — copy a summary, download as text or JSON, or print
